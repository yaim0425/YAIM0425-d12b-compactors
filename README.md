# YAIM904 d12b – Compactors 🗜️📦

---

## 📦 Description

**YAIM904 d12b – Compactors** introduces special machines that allow you to **compress items into compact forms** and later **decompress them back** to their original state.

This MOD is designed to make **storage, transport and logistics** more efficient, especially when dealing with large quantities of items.

---

## ⚙️ How it works (Player view)

* Adds **compactor entities** for item compression
* Items can be:

  * **Compressed** into dense forms for storage or transport
  * **Decompressed** back into normal items when needed
* Ideal for:

  * Saving storage space
  * Reducing belt or transport congestion
  * Long-distance logistics solutions

Operation is straightforward: insert items to compact them, or feed compacted items to restore them.

---

## ⚠️ Warnings

* 🚨 **This MOD alters the balance of storage and transport logistics**
* 📦 Makes handling large volumes of items much easier
* The images used for the machines in this MOD were taken from the MOD **Deadlock's Stacking Beltboxes & Compact Loaders**, created by **Deadlock989**.
* 🧪 Recommended for:

  * Large-scale factories
  * High-throughput logistics
  * Creative and sandbox gameplay
* ❌ Not recommended for strict vanilla-balance playthroughs
